HOW TO PLAY

🎭 Two players, one device

  • 🧙The Weather Master sees a secret weather. Keep it secret!
  • 📱Pass the device to the Weather Detective.
  • 🕵️The Detective reads the English clues and guesses the weather before time runs out.
  • 🎤After each case, the Master says the weather sentence out loud.

🌦️ 5 rounds

  • 🌤️Easy Weather — 3 simple clues.
  • 😎Emoji Weather — only emojis!
  • 📖Weather Scenario — read a short story.
  • 🕵️Mystery Weather — guess now or get another clue.
  • ⚡Weather Rush — 15 seconds, 6 answers, speed bonus!

🏆 Scoring

Correct immediately+100
Correct after the second clue+75
Correct after the third clue+50
⚡ Speed bonus (Round 5)+3 / second
🔥 3 correct in a rowSTREAK BONUS!
Wrong answer−25

⌨️ Keyboard: press 1–6 to answer, G = guess now, C = another clue, Esc = pause.
